Systems Design

Systems practice

A working reference for designing, engineering, governing, and analyzing systems across an enterprise estate of data and applications. Each card holds one idea with practice steps you can tick off, and field context on the back. Drag to reorder, flip any card, filter by area.

Four practices, one estate. Design sets the boundary and the shape. Engineering makes the result verifiable and operable. Risk, governance, and security decide what is permitted and what happens when it fails. Analysis keeps all of it tied to how the organization actually works.
